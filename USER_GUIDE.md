# Beer Path Tracker - Quick Start Guide

## What's New: Alcohol Percentage Tracking

The Beer Path Tracker now includes **accurate alcohol tracking** to calculate your real Blood Alcohol Content (BAC) and estimated sobriety time based on the actual beers you drink.

---

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Open the App
Visit the Beer Path Tracker in your browser.

### Step 2: Enter Your Personal Info (Top Right)
Before logging any stops, set your profile:

| Field | Options | Why It Matters |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| **Drink alert limit** | Default: 4 | Set your personal limit |
| **Body weight (kg)** | Your weight | Heavier body = lower BAC |
| **Body factor** | Male / Female | Different body water content |

> **Example:** 80kg male vs 65kg female drinking the same amount will have different BACs.

### Step 3: Log Your First Bar Stop
Fill in the form with:

#### Bar Information
- **Bar name**: "Murphy's Pub"
- **City / address**: "Stockholm" (or specific address)

#### Drink Information
- **Beers here**: "2" (how many beers at this stop)
- **Beer type / ABV %**: Select from dropdown

### Step 4: Choose Beer Type or ABV
This is the key new feature!

#### Quick Selection (Pre-defined types):
```
Lager / Pilsner (5%)     ← Standard beer
Light Beer (4.5%)         ← Lower alcohol
IPA (6%)                  ← Hoppy, more alcohol
Porter (5.5%)             ← Dark, medium alcohol
Stout (7%)                ← Heavy, more alcohol
Imperial Stout (9%)       ← Very heavy
Wheat Beer (4%)           ← Light
Strong Ale (8%)           ← Very strong
```

#### Custom ABV:
Select "Custom ABV %" and enter your specific percentage (0.5% - 20%)

**✓ Click "Log stop"**

## Important Tips

### 📍 Recording Your Route
As you add bars, the map shows your path. The app:
- ✓ Geocodes addresses (converts addresses to map coordinates)
- ✓ Calculates walking distance between bars
- ✓ Shows total distance traveled

### 📊 Tracking Over Time
The app calculates sobriety based on:
- Total alcohol consumed
- Time elapsed since first stop
- Your body weight and biological sex
- Standard metabolism rate (0.015% BAC/hour)

### 🍔 What Affects Your Real BAC
**The app assumes:**
- 500ml per beer
- Steady consumption
- Standard metabolism

**But real BAC also depends on:**
- ✗ Food in your stomach (slows absorption)
- ✗ Drinking pace (fast = higher peak)
- ✗ Your medications
- ✗ Your personal metabolism
- ✗ Carbonation level

> **This is an estimate for awareness, not a legal determination**

## Common Questions

### Q: Why is my BAC different than I expected?
**A:** The calculation depends on:
- Your exact body weight
- Time since your first drink
- Metabolism rate (0.015% per hour)
- The ABV of your specific beers

### Q: Can I use this to drive?
**A:** NO! This is not a legal determination. Always use a real breathalyzer and follow your local laws.

### Q: Will custom ABV remember?
**A:** Yes! Each stop stores its ABV. When you load the session, all custom values are restored.

### Q: What if I add the wrong ABV?
**A:** Remove that stop by clicking the "Remove" button next to it in the timeline, then add it again.

### Q: Does the app work offline?
**A:** Mostly! Geocoding (address lookup) requires internet, but viewing and logging stops works offline once addresses are found.

## Advanced: Understanding the Calculation

### How BAC is Calculated:
```
1. For each beer: Alcohol Grams = (ABV% ÷ 100) × 0.5L × 789g/L
2. Total Alcohol = Sum of all beers
3. Raw BAC = (Total Alcohol ÷ (Weight × 1000 × Factor)) × 100
4. Adjusted BAC = Raw BAC - (0.015 × Hours Elapsed)
5. Sobriety Time = Adjusted BAC ÷ 0.015
```

**Where:**
- ABV = Your beer's alcohol percentage
- 0.5L = Standard 500ml beer
- 789g/L = Alcohol density
- Factor = 0.68 (male) or 0.55 (female)
- 0.015 = Metabolism rate per hour
